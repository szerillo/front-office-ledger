Nov 14, 2026 (evening): display fixes

  • Share pages and preview images for every report card and every club page were rebuilt from the current grades. Before this, many showed numbers from an earlier build (for example the Yankees preview read -23.7 wins against -12.1 on the card, and Washington's read B against C+ on the card).
  • Report cards: the "Every trade in this tenure" table no longer calls the gap between its rows and the trade grade a rounding difference. It now states the gap and where it comes from.
  • Trade previews and the Heists list no longer apply a "win-now" weight (1.35x for contenders). The Ledger counts every win the same; the list now ranks on the plain wins gap.
  • Methodology: the split-half numbers and channel weights in the text now match the chart and the weights the grades use (the draft repeats most, at 0.26).
  • Raw transaction text no longer reads "traded player, [name]", and a cut-off "St. Louis Cardina" is fixed.
  • Winter links (#view=winters&id=2014-wsh) open any winter, not only those with a share page.

No grade or value changed in this update.

Nov 14, 2026: values under review

Values that depend on when a club's control of a player ended are labelled "under review" on 107 report cards, 320 winters and 19 winter share pages. Details.

Nov 12, 2026: rebuild and rule updates

Overall grades changed for CHC, MIL, PIT, PHI, SF and WSH; the 2014-15 Nationals and 2011-12 Athletics winters are now shown as too close to rank. Details.

Known open items (not yet fixed)

  • Two trades are recorded with a player on one side only: Milwaukee's May 2009 deal for Jody Gerut (Tony Gwynn Jr. went to San Diego) and the Angels' July 2012 deal for Zack Greinke (Ariel Peña and Johnny Hellweg went to Milwaukee). The trade tables include the missing players; the trade grades do not yet, so Milwaukee's trade total under Doug Melvin reads 4.1 wins too high and the Angels' for that season 1.1 too low.
  • Postseason series wins come from a schedule feed that misreads a few series. Example: the Yankees' 2003 and 2004 Division Series wins are missing and their 2011 Division Series loss is counted as a win. Success grades may move when this is fixed.
  • Some winter headlines read "Cash or a player to be named" or "Got [player]" when the club in fact sent or received players (for example the Yankees' 2018-19 Sonny Gray trade brought back Shed Long and a draft pick). These headlines are being re-checked.
  • A player released before a season starts is still charged to his old club for that season (see Nov 12).
  • Control-end values under review (see Nov 14).
