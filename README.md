# Front Office Ledger

A decision-level accounting system for sports front offices. Every draft pick, trade, signing, and waiver claim, graded against the market expectation for that transaction type and attributed to the executive regime that made it. Inspired by OOTP's Decision History screen; built for real MLB data first, schema designed to extend to other leagues.

## Current state (October 2026)

**Working today**
- `ingest/ingest_transactions.py` pulls the MLB Stats API transaction and draft feeds into SQLite/CSV. Verified coverage: reliable 2005 to present; Retrosheet's transaction file (1870s to 2020) is the planned historical backfill.
- `ingest/assemble_decisions.py` turns raw rows into decisions: trades grouped into deals, FA split major/minor, waiver claims vs losses, Rule 5, passive losses. 1,073 decisions assembled for the Elias regime (BAL), 524 for the Arnold regime (MIL). Person-less rows (picks, PTBNL, cash) route to a review queue.
- Entity resolution: 99.7% of MLBAM ids joined to the Chadwick Bureau register (`data/crosswalk.csv`), carrying B-Ref / FanGraphs / Retrosheet keys for the valuation pass.
- `engine/` holds the scoring methodology, proven on two proofs of concept: the Betts trade (three-lens scoring with retention rights) and the full Elias regime (draft slot curve, maturity schedule, all four channels).
- `prototype/index.html` is the product prototype: leaderboard, regime report cards, transaction trees, lens matrices, dark/light themes. Orioles card is scored end-to-end with real data; most other values are labeled sample data.

**THE 30-REGIME SWEEP (new):** every active MLB front office scored automatically. `ingest/sweep_ingest.py` pulls all 30 clubs' transactions from each regime's start (108k rows, floor 2005), `engine/sweep_score.py` values 13k players with Ledger WAR v0 and grades trades / drafts / waivers per regime (FA pending a contracts feed), and `ingest/integrate_sweep.py` splices the results into the prototype leaderboard. Regime table: `data/regimes.json`; results: `data/sweep_results.json`.

**IFA CHANNEL (new, sweep v1.2):** international amateur signings graded as their own channel. Signings auto-classified from the feed (minor-league deal, age <= 23 at signing, born outside draft territory, first pro appearance); realized value on the signing org graded against the league's realized value per signing class among observed regimes, which works because bonus pools are near-equal since 2017 (pre-2017 classes flagged uncapped). `engine/ifa_score.py`.

**FA CHANNEL GRADED (sweep v1.3):** 1,594 real contracts (ESPN free-agent tables 2006-2021, parsed by `engine/fa_grade.py` from `data/contracts_espn.csv`, plus curated majors for the 2022-2024 winters in `engine/contracts_recent.py`; verify pass complete, 58/58 exact matches against offseason trackers for the two fully-checked winters). Signings graded as realized LVM on the signing org minus contract-implied wins; regimes with fewer than 8 matched deals stay pending. All five channels now grade.

**DEV PILLAR (sweep v1.5):** player development is now its own top-level grade alongside FOR and Success. The archive: MLB Pipeline preseason top-100s, 2011-2026 (1,548 rank-rows, 823 players), pulled from MLB's data-graph GraphQL endpoint (`ingest/pull_ranks.py`); holding org per list derived from minor-league season rosters mapped to parent orgs (`ingest/dev_ingest.py`), so attribution is ours, not Pipeline's. Prospects are marked to market on a rank-to-surplus-wins curve; entry credit is net of acquisition cost (own draft pick vs the pick-value curve, own international signing, or acquired-as-prospect) with an inherited screen (a prospect developed to the list under a predecessor regime earns the new regime nothing at entry); year-over-year rank moves accrue to the org holding the player; graduation to MLB is a neutral exit; falling off without debuting is charged. Grades are league-relative because development is positive-sum (league rate: about +2.5 wins of prospect value per club-season). `engine/dev_score.py`; per-regime farm flows and current farm value included. Development credit stays home even when the prospect is later traded, which is the point.

**EXTENSIONS CHANNEL (sweep v1.6):** contract extensions graded as their own decision type, against the extension market for the player's service class rather than FA prices (the pre-arb discount is structural; picking which player to lock up, and at what price, is the skill). Class discounts of open-market $/win: pre-debut 0.35, early 0.45, arb 0.60, veteran 0.90 (documented assumptions, tunable when the book supports empirical fits). Curated book of ~60 major extensions inside current regime windows (`engine/contracts_ext.py`; the 2025-26 wave verified by web search: Guerrero 14/500, Crochet, Raleigh, Basallo, Anthony, Baz, Emerson, Griffin, Crow-Armstrong, Luzardo, Soderstrom, Wilson, Pratt). Deferral-heavy deals are costed at CBT/present value (Betts $306.7M PV, Ohtani $460.8M in the FA channel), and qualifying-offer signings now carry a draft-compensation charge on the FA ledger (confident QO list, flat second-round-pick value). Same-name collisions (two Jose Ramirezes, two Will Smiths) resolved by club fit. `engine/ext_grade.py`.

**RETROSHEET BACKFILL INGESTED:** the full 1873-2022 transaction archive (101,594 rows) is loaded as `retro_tx` and shipped in `data/retrosheet_tranDB.zip`. The information used here was obtained free of charge from and is copyrighted by Retrosheet. Interested parties may contact Retrosheet at www.retrosheet.org.

**SWEEP v2:** three rigor upgrades to every number on the site. (1) Trades now use control windows: value counts while the player stays with the club (6-season cap) and the window closes the first season he logs MLB time only elsewhere, so a re-signing is a new decision instead of leaking into the trade grade. (2) Win-curve leverage and time discounting apply sweep-wide on trades: each delivered season is weighted by L(w) at the receiving club's win total (wins delivered to a contender count up to ~1.8x, wins to a 70-win club ~0.65x) and discounted 10%/yr back to the deal date. (3) The Success grade now includes real postseason detail ingested from the league schedule feed 2005-2025 (`engine/postseason.py`, `data/postseason.json`): berths, series wins, pennants, and titles blend with W% and division titles. FA and waiver channels stay unweighted (cost and value accrue in the same seasons, so leverage cancels to first order).

**OCTOBER 2026 REFRESH + VALIDATION (v3.0):** full rebuild on final 2026 regular-season data (transactions through Oct 14; 2026 draft; 2025-26 FA winter graded with 36 web-verified contracts and six qualifying-offer charges; regime table updated: Mozeliak interim in Anaheim, Zoll interim in Minnesota; 2026 postseason detail through the LCS, disclosed as in progress). Two validation studies now ship in `engine/validation.py` and on the Methodology page. Accounting: across 327 team-seasons, roster-summed Ledger WAR v1 vs actual wins above a 48-win baseline gives r = 0.80 (R2 = 0.65), slope 0.84, MAE 6.75 wins. Prediction: across 134 pooled regime-years, net decision value per season predicts the NEXT two seasons' W% with partial r = 0.34 after controlling for the record to date (R2 0.28 -> 0.37). Decision quality is forward information the standings do not contain. The site now carries author attribution and a 'what this does inside a front office' section; regeneration is `ingest/regen_v3.py` (now versioned in-repo).

**METHODOLOGY v0.3, THE EMPIRICAL RENTAL MARKET:** 237 deadline rental deals classified exactly from the feed (acquired Jun-Aug, declared free agency within five months), receipt measured as rest-of-season Ledger WAR on the buyer, price as what the surrendered players actually became for the seller. Findings: a star rental win costs about 0.8 realized wins; the median deadline rental costs approximately nothing that ever materializes; over half of rental buys deliver under 0.3 wins. The trade lens stack consolidates to "paid vs the market at the time" plus "realized vs paid"; Boston's Betts return was market rate, and the criticism was never the price. `engine/rental_baseline.py`, `data/rental_market.json`, `docs/methodology-v03-addendum.md`. Recent-era ratios disclosed as right-censored while surrendered prospects mature.

**LEDGER WAR v1:** defense and baserunning joined into the value metric. Defense uses the SABR Defensive Index where published (finalist tables 2014-2022, full qualifier tables 2023-2025, parsed from sabr.org), falling back to Statcast fielding runs prevented (2016+) plus catcher framing runs (2015+) from Baseball Savant, all keyed on native MLBAM ids; baserunning uses Statcast runner runs (2016+). SDI includes framing for catchers, so it replaces rather than stacks with the Statcast components. Runs convert at each season's runs-per-win; multi-team seasons split proportionally. Pre-2014 seasons remain batting-plus-position only, disclosed. 6,180 player-seasons upgraded; gloves and catchers no longer run cold (Swanson +1.7 wins in 2023, framing lands for Raleigh and Bailey). `ingest/savant_ingest.py`, `ingest/sdi_ingest.py`, `engine/lvm_v1.py`.

**LEDGER WAR v1.5, PARK FACTORS (v3.1):** park factors joined into the value metric. For every team-year 2004-2026, the home/away run-environment ratio (runs scored plus allowed per game, both sides of the ball) from the Stats API's home/away splits, smoothed with a 3-yr centered rolling average and halved toward 1 to spread the home effect across a season played half on the road (`ingest/park_ingest.py`, `data/parks.json`; 690 team-years, mean 1.002, Coors ~1.15-1.19, Seattle ~0.91). Application is FanGraphs-style: hitters are docked (PF-1) x league runs/PA per plate appearance; pitchers' FIP is divided by PF before comparison to league. The full 25,589-player cache was rebuilt at v1.5 (`engine/lvm15.py`, `engine/lvm_v15_rebuild.py`). Validation after v1.5 + the archive universe: accounting r = 0.80 -> 0.86 (MAE 6.8 -> 5.2 wins; the archive sweep's 12,800 added role players fill out roster sums, and fuller rosters sum closer to team records, which is itself evidence of additivity), predictive partial r finishes at 0.338 (R2 0.282 -> 0.364). Arenado's Coors years cool by ~1.4 wins each; German Marquez's warm by about the same.

**ARCHIVE ERA, 71 HISTORICAL REGIMES (v3.1):** the same engine, generalized to regime windows with end dates, now scores every notable past front office 2005-2024 (`data/regimes_hist.json`: 71 curated regimes with start/end dates; `engine/hist_score.py`; full 2005+ re-ingest of all 30 clubs in `ingest/hist_ingest.py`, 244k transaction rows). Deals count only if dated inside the window; value realized after the regime ends still credits the decision (credit follows the decision-maker, the same philosophy as DEV's traded-prospect rule); draft maturity reads as of 2026. Trade/draft/waiver channels grade; FA is shown raw pending the historical contract backfill; DEV/EXT are not scored for past windows (the Pipeline archive and extension book cover active-era windows); all disclosed on each card. Regimes seated before the 2005 data floor are truncated and flagged with their true start (Epstein BOS from 2002-11, Sabean from 1996-09). The prototype's Era filter now serves real archive rows with full report cards and transaction trees: Sabean +191 net (that draft room: Posey, Bumgarner, the dynasty), Epstein +85 in Boston and +84 in Chicago, Stearns +98, Dombrowski Detroit +62 on trades, Daniels -113 over 15 years. Historical headline: **the best draft room of the data era is Sabean's San Francisco, +127 wins over slot.**

**RETENTION v2, IN THE COMPOSITE (v3.1):** retention graded against expectation instead of raw leakage. From every FA departure league-wide 2005-2023, the empirical curve E[next-3yr value elsewhere | last-3yr value here]; regimes graded on the gap between what their departures did and what departures of that caliber usually do, plus +1.0 win comp-pick credit per QO walkaway. Centered at zero by construction (league mean +0.1), so it joins the FOR composite. Post-2023 departures right-censored, excluded, disclosed. `engine/ret2.py`.

**METHODOLOGY v0.4, THE CONTROLLED-YEARS MARKET:** the rental study's complement: 1,372 deadline deals 2005-2023 with control measured from the feed (seasons to first FA declaration). One extra control year trades at ~1.0 paid/received (a real deadline premium above the 0.8 star-rental rate), two years ~0.86, and the 3+ bucket (prospect packages, the seller's seat) at ~0.37 with a 71% bust rate: sellers buy future wins at a third the price of present ones and pay in variance. `engine/controlled_market.py`, tables in `data/rental_market.json`.

**UNSIGNED-PICK SCREEN (v3.1):** a player who reappears in the draft table in a later year did not sign the earlier pick, so the earlier pick's realized value is zeroed (his later production with that club, if any, came through a separately graded decision) and its slot charge halved (the comp pick). Found the honest way: the Yankees dossier was crediting Gerrit Cole's 2020+ FA-era production to the 2008 pick that never signed.

**RETROSHEET BACKFILL JOINED, 1998-2008 (v3.2):** tracing the 2009 Yankees' title roster exposed that the MLB Stats API transaction feed is empty before 2009 (zero trades 2005-2008), so every 2005-2008 window had been draft-and-standings only. `ingest/retro_backfill.py` joins the Retrosheet archive (carried in the repo since the backfill was planned): 22,697 rows 1998-2008, players mapped retro->MLBAM via the full Chadwick register (`data/retro2mlbam.json`), team codes mapped to era-correct API names so value lookups land. Epstein's BOS trades fall to -26.5 (Beckett-for-Hanley enters the book), Dombrowski's DET rise to +94.9 (the Cabrera heist), Cashman's active window to +78.8. Validation stable. Data obtained free of charge from and copyrighted by Retrosheet (retrosheet.org). `docs/methodology-v32-addendum.md`.

**CHAMPIONSHIP PROVENANCE, ALL 21 TITLES (v3.2):** every WS winner 2005-2025 gets a computed roster ledger (core = >= 1.0 LVM in the title season, each piece traced to its acquisition event; inherited = pre-dates the regime's TRUE seat). Through lines on the Champions tab: median champion 88% self-built, title in year 7; 9 of 21 titles in year 10+; free agency a majority channel exactly once (2009 NYY); most inherited title 2023 TEX (85% Jon Daniels' core). `engine/champions_build.py`, `data/champs_computed.json`.

**TRADE MARKET COMPS (v3.2):** every graded trade's modal shows the three nearest deals by eventual value received, same season phase, from a 5,764-deal 1998-2023 buyer-seat archive, so each verdict reads against what deals of that shape usually cost. `engine/trade_comps.py`, `data/deal_archive.json`.

**THE 1998 FLOOR (v3.3):** the archive now reaches 1998: Retrosheet backfill extended to 1985 (provenance) with scoring floored at 1998; drafts 1998-2004 backfilled from the Stats API draft endpoint (`ingest/draft_backfill.py`); park factors to 1996 (pre-humidor Coors 1.26-1.31); 28,149-player value cache; Expos alias joined. 110 archive regimes (`ingest/regimes_hist_v2.py` documents the curation), Cashman's active window from his real 1998-02 seat. The complete Beane record: -28 net over 17.8 seasons. Sabean's full window: +226.

**28 CHAMPIONSHIP LEDGERS (v3.3):** every WS winner 1998-2025, each roster piece clickable into its onward chain. The era through-line: median title core 48% self-built 1998-2009, 90% 2010-2025. The inherited-core championship is going extinct. *(Corrected in v5.0.1: with 29 titles and the v4.8 roster accounting the split is 48% vs 74%, and seven of seventeen titles since 2010 came on majority-inherited cores, so the 'extinct' reading is withdrawn.)*

**VALIDATION v2 (v3.3):** predictive study pooled over 574 regime-years from 112 regimes; partial r 0.276 with cluster-bootstrap 95% CI [0.16, 0.37]; grouped 5-fold CV lifts out-of-sample R2 0.184 -> 0.245 on held-out regimes. Accounting (2015-2025 full-coverage era): r = 0.853, MAE 5.3 wins. `engine/validation.py`.

**COMPS v2 + SHAREABILITY (v3.3):** trade comps matched on control bucket + buyer posture; OG/Twitter cards with generated preview image, favicon, first-visit intro, mobile pass.

**LEDGER PROJECTION v1 (v3.4):** open deals carry a projected rest-of-window and projected final verdict beside the realized grade. Self-built, no licensed projections: delta-method aging curves from the 28k-player cache (hitters peak 27-28, the textbook shape recovered independently), Marcel-style 5/4/3 regressed base, control windows from the feed, prospects at Pipeline-rank expectation, unranked traded prospects at their empirical 0.41-win mean (n=1,327). Backtested: r = 0.62-0.67 vs actual next-season WAR. Grades never move on a forecast; the overlay is disclosed. `engine/proj_v1.py`, `engine/ages_fetch.py`, `data/proj_curves.json`.

**THE DEAL DESK (v3.5):** propose a move, get a verdict. A new tab evaluates hypothetical trades (players / ranked prospects / draft picks as pieces), FA signings, and extensions using the ledger's own machinery: Ledger Projection v1, win-curve leverage at current standing, market-rate check vs the 5,804-deal archive's price curves with bust rates, three nearest real comps with outcomes, counterparty DNA + head-to-head. Client-side sandbox; nothing recorded; grades never move on forecasts. This is the public proof of the decision-support claim: pointed at internal projections and pref lists, it is a deadline war room. `engine/desk_data.py`, `data/desk_data.json`.

**Methodology version: v0.2 base + v0.3 rental baseline + v0.4 controlled-years market + Ledger WAR v1.5 + archive sweep (1998 floor) + retention v2 + unsigned-pick screen + Retrosheet backfill + validation v2** (win-curve leverage, time discounting, retention rights, net-of-cost contracts, perspective rule). See `docs/methodology-v02-addendum.md`. The v0.3 target: empirical market-price baselines for trades (the rental-return curve) that consolidate the lens stack; see `docs/design.md` section 3.4b.

**Valuation: solved in-house.** Ledger WAR v1.5 (`engine/ledger_war.py` + `engine/lvm15.py`) is the self-computed open metric: wOBA batting + positional adjustments, FIP pitching anchored to league RA9, park factors, SDI/Statcast defense and framing, Statcast baserunning. B-Ref and FanGraphs block unlicensed programmatic access (verified); nothing here scrapes them, and the validation studies show the open metric holds (r = 0.80 against team records).

## Quickstart

    # refresh a team's transaction log (Orioles = 110)
    python3 ingest/ingest_transactions.py --team 110 --start 2018-11-01 --end 2026-07-21

    # pull draft classes
    python3 ingest/ingest_transactions.py --draft 2019 2020 2021 2022 2023 2024 2025

    # assemble decisions and show a deal from both sides
    python3 ingest/assemble_decisions.py --show "Corbin Burnes to Baltimore"

    # run the proofs of concept
    python3 engine/score_betts_trade.py
    python3 engine/elias_ledger.py
    python3 engine/methodology_v02.py
